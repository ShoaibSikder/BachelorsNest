Rental questions

How do I request a rental?

Create a bachelor account, open an available listing, and select Request Rent. You can follow its status in My requests.

Can I save a place for later?

Yes. Select the heart on a listing to keep it in Saved places while you compare your options.

When can I message the owner?

Once the owner accepts your rental request, open Messages to discuss the property and arrange the next steps.
